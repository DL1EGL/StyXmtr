# StyxMTR

StyxMTR turns an ADALM-Pluto into a transceiver for the QO-100 geostationary satellite and for 2 m, 70 cm, 23 cm and 13 cm. It is written by Robert Breuer, DL1EGL, the author of LogRunner, and runs on Windows and on the Raspberry Pi.

This repository holds the releases only. Download the installer from the [Releases](https://github.com/DL1EGL/StyxMTR/releases) page.

## What it does

- Receives and transmits SSB, CW, AM, FM, data modes (USB-D, LSB-D) and RADE (FreeDV) through a Pluto, either on this PC or through a PlutoTRX service on a Raspberry Pi at the dish.
- Locks onto the QO-100 beacon and keeps your frequency steady, so the LNB drift disappears.
- Shows a large waterfall, a detail view around the VFO and of the audio, an S-meter plot and the beacon itself.
- Reads the multimedia beacon of AMSAT-DL: DX spots, bulletins and the live page qo100info.html.
- Brings a voice keyer, VOX with anti-VOX, a microphone equalizer and a rigctld output for WSJT-X.

## Status

StyxMTR is in beta. Each beta works for 90 days after it was built. Feedback is welcome.
